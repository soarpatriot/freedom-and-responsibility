## Attribution and interpretation

The seven-row daily sequence, minimum version, if–then fallback, progress-log
format, and feedback rules are coaching adaptations of the book's methods.
They are not quoted instructions or a named seven-day program from the author.
The book's staged program spans at least three weeks; this skill supports the
user's request for repeatable seven-day cycles focused on their chosen habit.

Do not present the author's observations about wealth as proof that habits
alone cause wealth or that poverty, illness, or distress reflect bad character.
Avoid reproducing speculative neuroscience, genetic claims, fixed formation
deadlines, or anecdotal weight-loss results as established medical guidance.
The book itself gives varying formation times alongside its short-program
claims. Use seven days to observe, practice, and adjust; lasting change may take
many cycles. Do not invent statistics or promise the book's story outcomes.

Adapt examples to the person's situation: early waking must preserve adequate
sleep; savings targets must fit essential costs; activity must fit capability.
Encourage supportive boundaries without judging friends by wealth or mental
health. Acknowledge emotions and choose responses rather than demanding that
users eliminate negative feelings.
