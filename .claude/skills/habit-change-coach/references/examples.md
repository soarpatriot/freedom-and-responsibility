# Planning and progress examples

## New plan: scrolling after dinner

User: “I scroll for about two hours after dinner. I want to read more, but I
usually feel tired. Give me a seven-day plan.”

Possible habit map: after dinner → phone scrolling → easy relaxation. Relaxation
is a hypothesis to check. Replace the first few minutes of scrolling with an
enjoyable book rather than making the entire evening productive.

Provisional target: try five minutes of reading after dinner on five of seven
evenings; minimum version is one paragraph. Record actual scrolling minutes
separately to see whether reading changes the unwanted routine. The user
reported roughly two hours; do not infer more detailed baseline data.

Daily core action: after clearing the dinner plate, put the phone on a shelf,
sit with a prepared book, and read for five minutes or one paragraph if tired.
Then choose an intentional relaxing activity. Keep essential phone access.

| Day | Cue / when | Concrete action and amount | What to record |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | After dinner | Note what prompts reaching for the phone; put a book beside the chair and try the core action. | Trigger, reading amount, scrolling minutes. |
| 2 | After dinner | Repeat the core action; note whether scrolling was for rest, boredom, or something else. | Need/reward and actual amounts. |
| 3 | After clearing the plate | Repeat the core action and add it to the evening checklist. | Normal/minimum/not done; scrolling minutes. |
| 4 | Before dinner, then after it | Silence nonessential notifications for the reading period; repeat the core action. | Whether the setup helped; actual amounts. |
| 5 | After dinner when tired | If five minutes feels too much, read one paragraph, then rest; otherwise repeat five minutes. | Energy, chosen version, scrolling minutes. |
| 6 | After dinner, or after returning home if out | Repeat with a paperback; if the usual setting is unavailable, use one paragraph at home. | Context, obstacle, actual amounts. |
| 7 | After dinner; review afterward | Repeat the core action, then spend five minutes reviewing the recorded week. | Practice count, known scrolling amounts, and which setup to retain. |

Today: choose an enjoyable book and place it by the chair before dinner. If an
evening is missed, note the trigger and resume at the next dinner cue. At the
review, repeat the target if it is still effortful; increase reading slightly
only if it feels manageable. The aim is to learn what works, not to declare the
habit fixed after one week.

## Feedback: some success and a difficult evening

User: “On days 1 and 2 I read for five minutes and scrolled for 80 and 70
minutes. On day 3 I worked late, read nothing, and scrolled for two hours. I
think I failed.”

Suitable response:

“You tried the replacement on two of the three reported evenings. Your
scrolling was shorter than your usual roughly two hours on those two days;
we don't yet know whether that pattern will last. Working late may make the
five-minute step too demanding. That evening gives us something to adjust.

For day 4, keep the prepared book and phone shelf. If you arrive home late,
make one paragraph the target, then rest; you don't need to make up yesterday's
reading. Record when you got home, which version you tried, and scrolling
minutes. We'll see whether the smaller step works on tired evenings.”

Do not infer anything about days 4–7, invent a weekly success percentage, or
restart the cycle because of one missed evening.

## 中文完整示例：熬夜刷手机

用户：“我每天熬夜刷手机，经常到凌晨一两点，第二天很困。试过把 App 卸载，
但没几天又装回去了。给我一个七天的计划。”

回复要点：先用失败的尝试理解触发和奖赏，不要只是再让用户卸载一次。保护睡眠，
用一个现实的夜间线索，而不是要求“今晚就早睡两小时”。

可能的习惯链：睡前躺到床上（线索）→ 刷手机（惯常行为）→ 放松、逃避白天的
压力、怕错过消息（奖赏，待验证）。卸载之所以失败，很可能是因为它移走了行为，
却没有满足同一个需求。所以替代行为要能提供类似的放松，而不是单纯地“禁止”。

暂定目标：七天里有五个晚上，在上床前把手机放到够不到的地方（比如客厅充电），
床头改放一本纸质书或听十分钟音频；最小版本是只把手机拿出卧室、不一定读。
另外每天记录实际熄灯时间，用来观察刷手机的时长有没有变化。用户说“凌晨一两点”，
不要据此编造更精确的基线。

| 天 | 线索 / 何时 | 具体行动与分量 | 记录什么 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 睡前 | 留意是什么让你想刷手机；把充电器挪到卧室外，床头放好书或音频。 | 触发、熄灯时间、刷手机时长 |
| 2 | 睡前 | 重复核心行动；记录刷手机是为了放松、逃避，还是怕错过消息。 | 需求/奖赏与实际时长 |
| 3 | 上床时 | 重复核心行动，并把“手机出卧室”加入睡前清单。 | 正常/最小/未做；熄灯时间 |
| 4 | 睡前一小时 | 给手机设一个勿扰或定时断网；重复核心行动。 | 这个设置有没有帮助；实际时长 |
| 5 | 特别累或情绪差的晚上 | 如果读不进去，就只把手机拿出卧室然后休息；否则读十分钟。 | 精力、所选版本、刷手机时长 |
| 6 | 周末或作息被打乱时 | 作息变动也尽量保留“手机出卧室”这一步；分量可减到最小版本。 | 情境、障碍、实际时长 |
| 7 | 睡前；之后回顾 | 重复核心行动，再花五分钟回顾这一周的记录。 | 实践次数、已知熄灯时间、下周保留哪个设置 |

今天就做：睡前把充电器搬到卧室外，床头放好一本想读的书。某晚没做到，就记下
当时的触发，下一个睡前线索再继续，不必“补上”昨天。回顾时，如果这个目标还很
费劲，就重复它；只有当用户说已经轻松，才小幅增加。目标是弄清什么管用，而不是
一周后就宣布“戒掉了”。

### 中文反馈示例：部分成功 + 一个难熬的晚上

用户：“第 1、2 天我把手机拿出了卧室，大概十二点半睡；第 3 天加班回来太烦，
又躺床上刷到两点。我觉得我又失败了。”

合适的回复：

“你在报告的三个晚上里，有两晚完成了替代行动，而且那两晚比你平时的凌晨一两点
要早一些——这个趋势能不能持续，我们还不知道。第 3 天加班又烦，说明‘把手机拿
出卧室 + 读书’在高压的晚上可能太重了。那一晚正好给了我们可以调整的信息。

第 4 天，继续把充电器放在卧室外。如果又是很累很烦的晚上，就只做最小版本——
把手机拿出卧室然后直接休息，不要求读书，也不用补昨天。记录你几点到家、做了
哪个版本、熄灯时间。我们看看更小的那一步在难熬的晚上是否撑得住。”

不要推断第 4–7 天的情况，不要编造一周完成率，也不要因为一个晚上没做到就
把计划从头再来。

## Other situations to check during review

- “I procrastinate.” Offer a provisional small-start plan; ask which task and
  what happens before avoidance. Do not invent a work schedule or motivation.
- “我熬夜刷手机，试过卸载软件但又装回去了。” Reply in Chinese and use the
  failed attempt to explore the trigger and reward; do not just prescribe
  uninstalling again. Protect sleep while choosing a realistic evening cue.
  See the full Chinese worked example above for a complete plan and feedback
  round.
- “I met my target all week, but it took a lot of effort.” Keep the manageable
  practice or simplify it before adding habits; success does not imply ease.
- “I want to stop drinking; I shake when I don't drink.” Recognize possible
  dangerous withdrawal, recommend prompt medical support, and avoid a DIY
  seven-day detox schedule.
